# UniCart Privacy Policy

_Draft for review by the UniCart owner (and ideally a lawyer) before publishing. Last updated: 23 October 2026._

UniCart ("we", "us") is a campus group-buying service for university students in Nigeria. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. Contact: **unicartbytekena@gmail.com**.

## What we collect
- **Account details:** email address, password (stored only as a one-way hash), phone number, and the campus you belong to.
- **Student verification:** your university email address and one-time verification codes.
- **Orders:** the items you add to a lobby, their prices, payment status and payment references.
- **Payments:** card, bank and wallet details are entered on **Flutterwave**'s checkout. UniCart never sees or stores them. We keep the transaction reference, amount and status.
- **Security data:** a hashed form of your device/browser details and network address for sign-in protection, and audit records of payment events.
- **Campus selection:** the campus you pick when signing in, used for aggregate analytics.

## How we use it
To run your account and lobbies, verify you are a student, process and confirm payments, deliver and update you on your batch, prevent fraud and abuse, keep financial records, and understand usage in aggregate. We do not sell your data and we show no ads.

## Who we share it with
- **Flutterwave** to process payments.
- **Hosting and database providers** (Render) that store the data for us.
- **Google (Gmail)** to send verification and order emails.
- **Authorities** where the law requires it.

## How long we keep it
Account data is kept while your account is active. Payment and audit records are kept as long as required for accounting and fraud prevention, without your personal details once you delete your account. Expired sessions and codes are purged automatically.

## Security
Data travels over HTTPS. Passwords are hashed. Sessions can be revoked. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

## Your choices and rights
You can view and correct your details by contacting us, change your password in the app, sign out of all devices, and ask us to delete your data.

## Deleting your data
In the app choose **Delete my account** (lobby screen, "Your account" card) and confirm with your password. Your personal details (email, phone number, school email and password) are deleted **immediately** and you are signed out everywhere. Payment records we must keep for accounting are retained without your personal details, and unpaid items are removed. If you still have paid items in a batch that has not been delivered, we cannot delete the account until delivery; contact us and we will help.

You can also email **unicartbytekena@gmail.com** from your account email with the subject "UniCart account deletion request".

## Children
UniCart is for university students aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Changes
If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the app.
